TEXT: Gloranthan Classics The cult of Xiola Umbar teaches that all things have a soul which lives on after death. Xiola Umbar is associated with the Runes of Darkness, Harmony, and Fertility.

### CC:00395 | CC | p.218 | Organization
FIGURES: XIOLA UMBAR
TEXT: Organization No official organization oversees this cult. Whenever several High Priestesses come together, they decide upon the leader by counting the Rune spells of each. The priestess with the most Rune spell points wins. There is no official organization for Xiola Umbar 's temples. Each priestess guards and protects her charges. The terms Chief Priestess and High Priestess are used normally. Xiola Umbar is worshipped wherever trolls are found, though sometimes her cult is small. She is also worshipped by many humans throughout the world, often as a secondary deity. She is the traditional god of the weak and oppressed. There is no central stronghold of worship for her. The holiest place for this cult is in the Underworld, a fountain called Mirrordark, said to be where Xiola Umbar meditated and found her powers to cure. The holy days of Xiola Umbar vary with the season. The holy day always falls upon Harmony week, and the day always matches the seasonal element: Waterday of Sea season, Fireday of Fire season, Clayday of Earth season, Freezeday of Dark season, and Windsday of Storm season.
