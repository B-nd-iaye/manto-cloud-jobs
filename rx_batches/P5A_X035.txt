TEXT: The extended contest for Crossing the Underworld is simple to run. The Game Master should choose a starting obstacle and ask which hero is going to try to overcome it in a simple contest. After that, the Game Master chooses a new obstacle and asks for a different hero to overcome it. Each simple contest scores Resolution Points for the extended contest based on the level of success. This is repeated until either the heroes succeed or are lost in the Underworld (see So You Died in the Underworld). The individual heroes get benefits of victory or consequences of defeat based on their simple contests. The group gets a benefits or consequences based on the result of the extended contest. As this is a rising action, the heroes may suffer consequences of the extended contest even if the group is successful.
